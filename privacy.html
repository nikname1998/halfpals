Privacy Policy

For the app “Halfpals” and this website. Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short

Halfpals does not collect any data about you or your child. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools. The app does not connect to the internet. Everything that happens in the game stays on your device.

What data does the app process?

No personal data. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, age or location and needs no access to camera, microphone, photos or contacts. Touches on the screen are only evaluated while playing and are neither stored nor transmitted.

Children

Halfpals is made for children. Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

App Store

When you download Halfpals, Apple processes data (for example your Apple ID) under their own responsibility. If you agreed to it in your device settings, Apple may show us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as download or crash counts). We cannot identify anyone from them. See Apple’s privacy policy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address as technically necessary and keeps it briefly in server logs for security. We do not see this data. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no fonts or content from other providers. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact and bug reports

Privacy questions or bug reports: motoapplications [at] proton.me. There is no form: you simply write us an email from your own mail app. We only use your email address and message to reply and fix the bug, and delete it afterwards. The app itself never sends anything automatically. Our mailbox is hosted by Proton (Proton AG, Switzerland). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, to object, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Changes

If anything about Halfpals changes in a way that affects data (for example advertising), we will update this policy beforehand and point it out in the app.
